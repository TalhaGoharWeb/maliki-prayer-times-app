Privacy Policy — Maliki Prayer Times

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · App version 1.3.8 · Developer: Muhammad Talha Bin Farid · Contact: muhaqqiqcreates@gmail.com

Maliki Prayer Times calculates prayer times on your phone. It has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry. This policy describes what the app collects, stores, transmits — and what it never touches. Anything not listed here does not happen.

Location

Location is used only when you ask for it: when you tap the device-location button, or refresh a device location. The app takes a one-shot GPS fix (medium accuracy). There is no background tracking, no continuous location watch, and no location access at startup.

After a successful fix, your coordinates are sent to your phone's own reverse-geocoder (a platform service) to obtain a human place name such as a city. That lookup is bounded by a 10-second timeout; if it fails, the app simply shows "Device location". The place name is stored on your phone alongside the coordinates.

You can always enter a location manually instead — latitude and longitude typed by you, with no permission needed.

What stays on your phone only

Everything the app remembers lives on your device and is never transmitted anywhere by the app:

  • Your chosen location (name, latitude, longitude, place name), saved places, and the notification schedule's inputs;
  • Calculation profile, precautionary-delay (ihtiyat) setting, per-prayer minute adjustments, notification and reminder settings, and the app language;
  • Qada (missed prayer) records and Hayd/Nifas (menstruation and postnatal bleeding) records. Bleeding records are kept in the phone's secure storage. They are never sent anywhere, and you can delete all Qada data from inside the app;
  • A flag recording that the notification permission prompt was shown.

There is no server-side profile and no cloud sync.

Network use

Google Play build: the app makes no network request of its own. Updates come from Google Play, and the in-app "check for updates" feature is disabled in this build.

Builds installed outside Google Play: tapping "check for updates" in Settings fetches the latest release information from the project's public GitHub releases page (api.github.com). Nothing is checked at startup, in the background, or on a timer.

Prayer-time calculation itself is pure computation on your phone and works fully offline.

Notifications

Prayer notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. No push service is used, and no notification content ever leaves your phone.

Permissions used (Android)

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — one-shot device-location fix, only when you ask for it.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — prayer-time and reminder notifications (you are asked at first launch).
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — so prayer alarms can fire at the exact time when you grant them; otherwise reminders may arrive late, and the app says so honestly.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-schedules your prayer notifications after the phone restarts.
  • INTERNET — only for the manual update check in builds installed outside Google Play; unused in the Google Play build.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, ads, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • No account, no email or contact collection, no photos, no files.
  • No secrets: no API keys, tokens or credentials in the app.
  • No coordinate logging: precise coordinates are never written to logs.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes everything it stored. Qada data can also be deleted from inside the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change. The version of the app this policy describes is shown at the top of the page.
